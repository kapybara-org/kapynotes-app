Kapy Notes 1.35.2

- A new layout for iPhone. Each screen is its own page, and a note opens over the list. Swipe from the edge to go back.
- The bar at the bottom has Notes, Tasks, Drawings and Settings, and + makes a note, a task or a drawing.
- Notes shows your notes a day at a time, with Pinned and Shared cards at the top and your streak by the title.
- Share from one sheet: add someone by email and choose Can edit or Can view. Sharing is also in a note's menu, and a shared note shows who it's shared with.
- Settings has a Sharing section with your invitations and everyone you share with.
- Block and Leave do exactly what they say, and when a note is shared together with other notes, Leave says how many notes you're leaving. Removing someone or stopping sharing asks first, and Undo puts it back.
- If a note is shared with you to view only, you can remove it from your notes without leaving the share.
- A task can hold details: indent lines under it for text, pictures or smaller tasks. Tap a task to open them.
- Clearer icons, calmer text, and note titles and tasks that stay readable at any text size.
- An edit someone else made that your iPhone couldn't open yet is no longer lost, and a note shared again with the same people keeps updating for them.
