Day tabs and easier folders.

- A day with several notes opens them as tabs. Pin or drag a tab, and the order stays on all your devices.
- Swipe the week under a note to change weeks.
- Add notes to a folder from its page.
- Notices no longer let taps through or cover the Share sheet's buttons.
- TalkBack can now tap Home, the day button and a day's note count.
- With large text, Quick find's buttons show their whole words.
- A camera photo is kept if Android closes the app while taking it.
