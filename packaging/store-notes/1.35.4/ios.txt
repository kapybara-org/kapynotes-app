Kapy Notes 1.35.4

- The bar at the bottom has Notes, Folders and, when it's on, Kapy AI. Tap + for a new note, or press and hold it for a task, voice note or drawing.
- Tap Tasks at the top of Home, or swipe, to see your tasks, and tap your picture for Settings. Drawings are in Folders.
- Home shows Pinned and Shared as cards, with your notes under each day.
- Give a note a later day and it waits in Folders under Upcoming, named Tomorrow, Tue 24 or 7 Dec.
- A new note page: the week sits at the top, that day's tasks are a tap away, and Move to another day opens a month you can swipe through. Tap an empty day to start that day's note.
- Kapy AI opens from a note as a sheet, so the note stays in view.
- Tasks has search and a filter, and a task opens on its own page.
- Settings opens on a short list of simple rows.
- The widgets have a fresh look.
- Undo after removing someone from a shared note keeps them in it, or invites them again if it's too late. Notices sit above the bar.
- Times no longer start with a zero.
