Recording with the screen off, and a swipe for each note.

- Recording keeps going with the screen off, with Pause and Stop in its notification.
- Swipe a note left to pin or delete it. A full swipe archives it, with Undo.
- Back goes one step back from anywhere.
- Pinned notes sit in a row on Home, and Shared is in Folders. Upcoming is gone.
- Transcribing on your device no longer skips sentences.
- About & updates shows your version and opens Google Play when an update is waiting.
